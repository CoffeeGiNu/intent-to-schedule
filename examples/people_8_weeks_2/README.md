# people_8_weeks_2

One synthetic team of eight people over two weeks.

- `calendar.json`: input for `init --calendar`. People, working hours, and 82 existing appointments. The horizon runs from Monday 2026-10-12 to Saturday 2026-10-24 in `+09:00`, with 30-minute slots.
- `intents.en.txt`, `intents.jp.txt`: a scheduling survey and one reply per person, in English and Japanese. The replies are the requests to turn into commands. A few, such as a cap on new meetings per day, have no direct command.
